How to play Connections

Find 4 groups of 4 related words.
Tap to select tiles, then press Submit when you think you’ve found a group.

  • You can only submit exactly 4 tiles at a time.
  • You get up to 4 strikes. A wrong group uses a strike.
  • When you solve a group, it locks to the top.
