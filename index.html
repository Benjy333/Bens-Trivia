How Brisbane's Smartest Venue works

  1. Pick your venue. Scan the QR code at the venue to play for it. A "Playing for" badge at the top of the page confirms it.
  2. Play the weekly quiz. Complete the 25 question quiz and submit your score. Only your first score each week counts.
  3. Venues are ranked by average score. Every counted score at a venue is averaged into a percentage.
  4. 30 completed quizzes to be eligible. A venue needs at least 30 completed quizzes across the 6 weeks to qualify for the title.
  5. The winner is crowned on the 1st of December 2026. The qualifying venue with the highest average wins.

Runs 18th of October to 28th of November 2026.
